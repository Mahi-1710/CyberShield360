Cyber Safety Center

Quick security practices for everyday digital safety.

🔐 Password Safety

Use long and unique passwords. Avoid using the same password on multiple accounts.

🎣 Phishing Protection

Do not click suspicious links. Verify the sender and website before entering sensitive information.

📱 OTP & UPI Safety

Never share OTP, PIN, CVV or UPI PIN with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank.

🌐 Safe Browsing

Prefer HTTPS websites and carefully check the domain name before logging in.

💻 Device Security

Keep your operating system, browser and security software updated.

🚨 After a Scam

Secure affected accounts immediately, change passwords and contact the relevant official service.
